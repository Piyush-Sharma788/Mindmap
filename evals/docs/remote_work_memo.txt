To: All staff
Subject: Hybrid work policy from next quarter

After a six-month pilot, we are moving to a hybrid work model. Teams will be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely on the other three days.

Why we are changing. The pilot showed that focused work such as writing and analysis went better at home, while planning, onboarding and problem-solving sessions went better in person. Employee surveys showed higher satisfaction, and voluntary attrition fell from 14 percent to 9 percent.

What changes. We will reduce our office lease by one floor, which saves roughly 400,000 dollars a year. Part of that saving will fund a home-office stipend of 500 dollars per employee, paid once. Desks will no longer be assigned; staff will book them through the new booking app.

Expectations. Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm in your local time zone. Managers will set team agreements about response times and meeting-free blocks. Performance will be judged on outcomes, not on hours online.

Security. Remote staff must use the company VPN and managed laptops. Customer data may not be stored on personal devices.

We will review the policy after two quarters, using the same survey and attrition measures as the pilot.
